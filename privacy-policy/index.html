Readme

This Privacy Policy applies to the personal website available at https://tenshi.qzz.io/ (hereinafter referred to as "this Website"), operated by GitHub@緋想天子.

This Website is a purely static fan site published through GitHub Pages. It has no backend, no database, no user accounts, no comment system, no analytics and no advertising. Almost everything described below follows from that single fact: there is no server of ours that could store your data, and there is no third-party tracking script embedded in any page.

All opinions expressed on this Website are the operator's own personal views. They do not represent, and are not endorsed by, any employer, organisation, institution or other entity, and nothing on this Website creates any relationship with such a body.

This Website fully recognises the importance of your personal information and is committed to safeguarding its security and reliability. We strive to maintain your trust by adhering to the following principles in protecting your personal information: accountability, purpose specification, consent, data minimisation, security safeguards, individual participation, and transparency. Furthermore, we pledge to implement appropriate security measures in line with industry standards to protect your data.

To ensure optimal protection of your personal information, we recommend that you carefully review this updated Privacy Policy. Should you have any questions or objections regarding its terms, please contact us using the details provided in section 18.

Respect for Your Privacy

This Website respects and protects the privacy of all users. We collect as little information as technically possible, and we do not build profiles, we do not sell data, and we do not share data with advertisers. This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically; the current version and the date of the last change are always shown at the top and at the end of this page. By agreeing to our Terms and Conditions, you are deemed to have accepted all terms of this Privacy Policy, which forms an integral part of those Terms.

1. Scope of Application

This Privacy Policy applies to all pages of this Website, including its home page, the Contact, Tools, Download and About pages, the password-protected content vaults described in section 10, the encrypted downloads described in section 11, and the background music player described in section 12.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to:

  • third-party websites reached through links on this Website — their own privacy policies apply (see sections 13 and 14);
  • the hosting infrastructure operated by GitHub Pages — no visitor data is stored by us there, but GitHub processes technical request data under its own privacy statement (see section 13);
  • your browser, operating system, extensions or network provider — these are outside our control.

2. Use of Information

Browsing this Website does not require an account and does not require you to provide any personal information. We do not create user profiles and we do not track you across pages or across visits. The only situations in which any information reaches us at all are:

  • When you send us an email, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use them solely to answer your enquiry or to process your request (for example a correction or removal request), and for nothing else.
  • When you use the password-protected vaults or the download page, the password you type and the content it unlocks stay inside your browser (see sections 10 and 11). They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.
  • When you interact with the background music player, your browser loads audio files from this Website; there is no "listening activity" reporting and nothing is uploaded (see section 12).

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information. We do not use personal information for advertising, retargeting, profiling, automated decision-making or marketing communications.

3. Disclosure of Information

Because this Website does not collect or store personal information, there is in practice no personal information to disclose. We will nevertheless disclose information only in the following limited cases, and only to the extent required:

  • with your prior consent, when you ask us to do so;
  • when required by applicable law, a court order, or a lawful request from a competent authority;
  • when necessary to investigate abuse, to protect the security of this Website, or to defend our legal rights;
  • to our hosting provider GitHub Pages strictly as part of delivering the requested web pages (see section 13).

We do not sell personal information to data brokers or to any other third party.

4. Information Storage and Transfer

Nothing is stored server-side by us. This Website consists of static files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images, fonts, audio) that are delivered to your browser by GitHub Pages. There is no database, no application server and no account system in which information about visitors could be retained.

  • Encrypted vaults and encrypted downloads are static files; the decrypted plaintext exists only in the memory of your own browser tab (section 10 and section 11).
  • Emails you send us are kept in our ordinary email mailbox only for as long as needed to deal with your request, and are deleted on request (section 16).
  • We do not transfer visitor data across borders, because we do not collect visitor data. The static files are served from GitHub's globally distributed infrastructure, which may be located outside your country; that is a property of the host, not a transfer of any data about you by us.

5. Use of Cookies

  • This Website uses exactly one kind of cookie: the theme-preference cookie written when you use the light/dark theme switch (see section 9 for its exact name, value, lifetime and attributes).
  • We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting scripts, or third-party cookies of any kind. No consent banner is shown because no consent-gated cookie is set.
  • You may accept, refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. If you block or delete the theme cookies, the Website still works; it simply follows your operating-system colour-scheme preference again.
  • Information obtained via cookies is subject to this Policy.

6. Information Security

  • This Website is served over HTTPS by GitHub Pages. There is no login, no password database and no server-side storage to breach.
  • The password-protected vaults and encrypted downloads use modern cryptography performed locally in your browser (PBKDF2-SHA256 with 210,000 iterations and AES-256-GCM for page vaults; AES-256-CBC with per-file integrity verification for downloads). Your password, the derived keys and the decrypted content never leave your browser and are never written to cookies or to browser storage (sections 10 and 11).
  • However, no internet system is entirely infallible, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for the password you use for the vaults and for keeping your own device and browser up to date.
  • If you discover a security problem, please report it to shameimaru.ayaaya@gmail.com instead of exploiting it.

Protecting Your Personal Information

To prevent unauthorised disclosure, we urge you to safeguard any personal details you choose to share (for example in an email) and to share them only when necessary. In particular, do not reuse an important password for the vaults described in section 10; those passwords protect hidden page content and files, not identity, and they cannot be recovered if lost. If you suspect a breach involving a password you have used here, change that password wherever else you have used it and notify us at shameimaru.ayaaya@gmail.com for prompt action.

8. No Accounts and No Analytics

This Website has no registration, no login, no member area and no user profiles. You never create an account here, and we never ask for a name, address, phone number or password to be submitted to us.

This Website also contains no analytics or tracking of any kind: no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Umami, no server-side tracker, no advertising network, no social-media embed that reports back to its platform, no CDN or font service that receives your IP address. All CSS, JavaScript, images, fonts and audio are served from this Website's own origin. The only remote URLs in the page source are ordinary hyperlinks and the social-share preview image, which is fetched by link-preview crawlers, not by your browser.

The counters shown on the home page (anime/game/book statistics and repository information) are static copies of public data. They are refreshed periodically by the site operator's own automation and stored as ordinary files on this Website; your browser loads only that static copy and never contacts the data sources directly (section 13).

9. The Theme Preference Cookie

This Website writes two small first-party cookies, and only when you click the light/dark theme button in the navigation bar:

  • themeState — the theme you selected (Light or Dark);
  • themeMode — whether that choice is manual (manual) or should follow your system preference (auto).

Attributes: both are first-party cookies for this domain, with Path=/, SameSite=Lax, a lifetime of 365 days, and they are served only over HTTPS. They contain no identifier, no timestamp, no personal data and no analytics value. They are written by page JavaScript (so they are not HttpOnly), and they are read before first paint only to avoid a flash of the wrong theme. Clearing your browser's cookies for this site removes them; the Website then returns to following prefers-color-scheme.

No other cookies are set, and the pages do not use localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB to store information about you. The BGM player's temporary state (current track, playback position, volume) exists only in the memory of the open page.

10. Encrypted Content Vaults

Some pages — currently About and Download — contain a "vault": part of the page is encrypted and is shown only after you type the password that the site owner shared with you out of band.

  • Everything happens in your browser. The encrypted payload is a static file (vault.json); the page fetches it only after you submit a password, not while you are just reading the page.
  • Key derivation and decryption are local. The page derives a key from your password with PBKDF2-SHA256 (210,000 iterations, random salt stored in the payload) and decrypts with AES-256-GCM using the Web Crypto API built into your browser.
  • Nothing is sent to a server. The password, the derived key and the decrypted plaintext are never transmitted, logged, or written to cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB. The plaintext is inserted into the page's DOM in memory so that you can read it; it disappears when you click "重新锁定" (re-lock) or close/reload the tab.
  • Wrong passwords fail locally. AES-GCM authentication makes a wrong password produce a local "密码不正确" message; no verification request is sent anywhere.
  • No recovery. We do not know your password and cannot recover it or the content for you.
  • Use a unique password. The vault password is a shared secret for hidden content, not an account credential; do not reuse an important password for it.

11. Encrypted File Downloads

The Download page offers files that are stored encrypted and are decrypted locally after you unlock the page vault and enter the file password:

  • Each encrypted file is a static file served from this Website (under /static/resources/), encrypted with AES-256-CBC. Its IV and a SHA-256 integrity value are served as small sibling static files (keys/…iv and hash/…hash).
  • The key is derived in your browser from the password you type (SHA-256 of the password combined with a site constant) using the Web Crypto API. The file is decrypted in memory, its integrity is checked against the SHA-256 value, and the result is offered to you as a normal download link created with a temporary browser object URL.
  • Nothing you type and nothing that is decrypted is uploaded. The Website operator cannot see your password or which files you unlock.
  • Downloaded content may be protected by third-party copyright or other rights (section 15). You may use it for personal purposes only and must not redistribute it.
  • Because the files are decrypted on your own device, you download and use them at your own risk; we cannot guarantee that they will suit any particular purpose.

12. Background Music Player

The site includes a small music player for background listening. It is entirely self-hosted:

  • The playlist (/static/bgm/playlist.json) and the audio files and cover images it references are ordinary static files served from this Website. They are fetched only after you interact with the player — the page does not preload any audio for you.
  • Playback starts only when you press play; there is no autoplay, no audio recording and no upload of any kind. The player does not report listening activity anywhere.
  • No third-party music service is involved at runtime, and no account or login is needed.
  • The recordings and cover art are the property of their respective performers, labels and artists. They are provided for personal, non-commercial listening only; no licence is granted to redistribute them, and rights holders may request removal (section 15).

The site itself embeds no third-party scripts, fonts, players or trackers. The following third parties are nevertheless relevant to how the site is delivered or what it displays:

  • GitHub Pages (hosting). This Website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you request a page, GitHub's infrastructure necessarily processes technical request data (such as your IP address, user agent and requested URL) in order to serve the files and to protect the service. We, the site operator, do not receive a visitor log from GitHub Pages and do not process that data. GitHub's own Privacy Statement applies to that processing.
  • GitHub (links and previews). Links to github.com/Hisou-Tenshi are ordinary hyperlinks. The social-share preview image declared in the page metadata is hosted on avatars.githubusercontent.com; it is fetched only by link-preview crawlers when the page is shared, not by your browser while reading the page.
  • Bangumi (public collection counters). The statistics shown on the home page are copied from the site owner's public Bangumi collection. The refresh is performed by the site owner's scheduled automation and the resulting numbers are stored as a static file (static/data/bangumi.json); your browser does not contact bgm.tv to display them. Bangumi's own policies apply to that server-side request.
  • GitHub repository metadata. In the same way, public repository information is captured periodically into static files (for example static/data/repos.json); no request is made from your browser to GitHub's API to display it.
  • Linked communities. Links to x.com, facebook.com, instagram.com, thwiki.cc, bgm.tv and the other sites listed in the "Sites" section are ordinary hyperlinks. If you follow them, those sites apply their own policies and may set their own cookies.

We do not use advertising networks, payment processors, analytics providers, social embeds or any other service that would receive data about your visit.
