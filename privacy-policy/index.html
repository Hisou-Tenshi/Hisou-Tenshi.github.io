Readme

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit tenshi.qzz.io or the same personal website at hisou-tenshi.github.io ("this Website"). The operator is GitHub@緋想天子 (Hisou-Tenshi), reachable by email as set out in section 18.

At a glance: this is a static personal fan site with no visitor accounts, advertising or site-operated behavioural analytics. The site uses two theme cookies and a short-lived navigation marker in your browser. Hosting and delivery providers process technical requests; a Tools illustration is loaded directly from GitHub. If you email us or participate in the source repository, the information you choose to send is processed as described below.

This Policy is a notice about processing, not a request for blanket consent. Reading the Website, accepting its Terms and Conditions, or continuing to browse does not by itself constitute consent to processing that requires consent under applicable law.

Respect for Your Privacy

We minimise the information we handle and use it for the purposes explained here. We do not sell or rent personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or build visitor profiles. The lack of an application backend does not mean that visiting the site is anonymous to its infrastructure providers.

1. Scope of Application

This Policy covers the pages, browser tools, music player, encrypted content and downloads operated as part of this Website, together with related enquiries to the operator. It distinguishes our handling of correspondence from processing by GitHub, Cloudflare and email providers.

Independently operated projects, including services linked from Tools, and other external websites have their own policies. A project linked here is not automatically covered by this Policy, even if its maintainer is the same person. Your browser, extensions, device and network provider may also process data under their own settings and policies.

2. Information We Handle and Why

  • Delivering pages and files: infrastructure providers receive technical data such as an IP address, requested URL, request time and browser headers. These are used to deliver content, cache resources, diagnose failures and protect the service. See section 13.
  • Browser preferences and interactions: theme choices, navigation effects, search text, calculator inputs, music controls and vault passwords are handled by the relevant page code as described in section 9 and Local Interactions.
  • Correspondence: if you send an email, we receive your address, message, attachments and associated email headers, and any name or other details you include. We use them to answer you, handle privacy or copyright requests, investigate reported defects or abuse, and keep necessary records of that work.
  • Repository participation: GitHub Issues, pull requests and commits can expose your account name, comments and commit metadata publicly. See Open Source and Public Contributions.

We do not use these details for marketing, automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects, or unrelated profiling. Where a legal basis is required, we rely on the basis applicable to the activity: for example, legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and maintaining a secure site where that basis is available, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where required. Any consent required for a new optional purpose must be obtained separately; this notice does not supply it.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

Hosting, delivery and email providers process information needed to operate their services. We may also disclose correspondence or incident information with your direction or consent, to comply with applicable legal requirements, or where necessary and lawful to investigate abuse or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We limit disclosure to what is needed for the purpose.

We do not publish private emails without permission, except where disclosure is required by law. Public repository submissions are already visible to others and may be copied, indexed, forked or archived. We do not sell information to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Storage and International Processing

The public site consists of static files. We do not operate a visitor account database or a form-submission backend. Correspondence is nevertheless stored in the operator's email account, and repository submissions are stored on GitHub.

GitHub, Cloudflare and email providers use infrastructure that may process data outside your country. This can include technical request data and correspondence; we therefore do not promise that information remains in your country or that no international processing occurs. Their privacy notices describe their locations, transfer arrangements and safeguards. Contact us if you need clarification about information we handle directly.

Browser caches, session storage and files that you save are controlled by your browser or device. See section 16 for retention and deletion limits.

5. Cookies and Browser Storage

The site's own scripts write two first-party theme cookies, themeState and themeMode, and use one sessionStorage key, shrine-arrival, for a navigation animation. These are functional settings, not visitor identifiers or a browsing-history database. Their values and lifetimes are described in section 9.

We do not add analytics or advertising cookies. Infrastructure providers may separately use cookies or request processing for service delivery and security under their own policies. You can inspect, block or clear cookies and site storage in your browser; theme memory or navigation effects may stop working, while public pages remain readable. We will assess any new storage or tracking use and obtain consent where required before enabling it.

6. Information Security

The public site uses HTTPS. The current vault code decrypts in the browser and does not upload passwords, derived keys or decrypted bytes. This does not remove risks from compromised browsers, extensions, devices, hosting or weak shared passwords.

Page vaults use PBKDF2-SHA256 (currently 210,000 iterations) and AES-256-GCM. File downloads use an older SHA-256-based key derivation and AES-256-CBC format with a separate ciphertext hash. These schemes have different protections; the file format must not be treated as equivalent to authenticated AES-GCM encryption. See section 10 and section 11.

Report vulnerabilities privately using the email in section 18. Avoid posting passwords, decrypted private content or other people's data in a public issue. Where applicable law requires notice of a personal-data breach, we will follow those requirements.

7. Changes to This Policy

We publish revisions here with an updated date and a Change Log. Material changes will be highlighted on this page. If a new activity requires consent or another notice under applicable law, we will provide it before that activity begins. A revision does not retrospectively authorise an unrelated use of information already received.

Protecting Your Personal Information

Share only what is needed for your enquiry. Do not send identity documents, account passwords, payment details or other sensitive information unless a lawful request genuinely requires it and an appropriate method has been agreed. Vault passwords are shared access secrets rather than personal accounts; do not enter a password you use elsewhere.

If you use a shared device, clear form inputs, close unlocked pages and remove any saved downloads when finished. Re-locking a page is not secure erasure of browser memory or files.

8. No Accounts or Site-Operated Analytics

There is no visitor registration, login, public comment system, file-upload form, advertising network or site-operated behavioural analytics. The Contact letter form prepares an email locally; it is not a submission endpoint.

Public Bangumi collection counters and GitHub repository information are refreshed by the operator's automation into static JSON files. Displaying these snapshots does not make your browser contact the Bangumi or GitHub APIs. This statement does not cover the separate GitHub-hosted image on Tools, GitHub download links, infrastructure logs or Cloudflare network-error reporting described in section 13.

9. Theme Cookies and the Navigation Marker

  • themeState: Light or Dark, written when you choose a theme.
  • themeMode: manual when you choose a theme, or auto when you select Auto to follow your system.

Both are written by page JavaScript with Path=/, SameSite=Lax and an expiry 365 days after the relevant setting is written. They have no account or visitor identifier and are readable by scripts, rather than HttpOnly. As cookies, they can accompany requests to this origin. Each site domain has its own settings. Deleting both restores the default of following prefers-color-scheme.

sessionStorage["shrine-arrival"]: when you follow a recognised same-origin page link, the navigation script can store the destination page, its path and a timestamp to trigger an arrival animation. The destination reads and removes the marker; it only triggers an animation if less than 30 seconds old. The 30-second check is not an automatic storage expiry: an unread marker can remain for the browser's page session until removed or cleared. The script does not send it to a server or accumulate a history.

The current site scripts do not use localStorage or IndexedDB for visitor data. Browser caching and session restoration are separate browser features and can persist data according to your settings.

10. Encrypted Content Vaults

The About and Download pages contain encrypted page fragments. Submitting an access password fetches a static vault.json payload and attempts local PBKDF2-SHA256 key derivation and AES-256-GCM decryption. The payload may be cached in the open page. Requesting it sends an ordinary file request to the delivery providers, even if the password is wrong.

The unlock code does not submit passwords, keys or decrypted text to us and does not write them to cookies or browser storage. Successful decryption inserts the text into the page. Re-locking removes that displayed fragment and clears the page-vault password input; it does not guarantee memory erasure or revoke copies, screenshots, downloads or browser-restored content. Any links or resources you open from the unlocked content can generate their own requests.

Encrypted files are publicly retrievable ciphertext, so password guessing can take place offline. Access depends on having the correct shared secret, not on a server checking your identity. We provide no personal-account password-reset service.

11. Encrypted File Downloads

After unlocking Download, you may request individual encrypted files. The file, its IV and a separate SHA-256 value are fetched as static resources. The current code derives an AES-256-CBC key from SHA-256 of the supplied password and a site constant, checks a ciphertext hash when supplied, decrypts locally, performs basic filename checks, and creates a browser object URL for saving the result.

The hash detects a mismatch with the supplied ciphertext hash; it is not a digital signature, a malware scan, or proof of the decrypted file's authenticity. Because the hash and file come from the same delivery system, this is not independent protection against replacement of both. The file scheme does not have the authenticated-encryption guarantees of the page vault.

The code does not upload the password or decrypted bytes. Providers can still process the encrypted file's request path and technical metadata. A file you save stays on your device until you delete it and may be included in your backups. Re-locking a vault does not delete that file or guarantee immediate release of existing object URLs or browser memory. Copyright and redistribution depend on the file's actual licence; see the download terms.

12. Background Music Player

The custom player requests its static playlist after interaction and serves its current audio from this Website. It does not automatically play music on arrival, record your microphone, or send listening-event analytics. Selecting a track or starting playback can generate audio requests that delivery providers process like other file requests.

Track selection, playback position and volume are temporary page state in the current implementation. Browser or operating-system media features may handle playback separately. The recordings remain subject to their rights holders' permissions; the site's software licence does not license the music.

13. Third-Party Services and Hosting

  • GitHub Pages: serves the static site. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security even when they are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not operate a visitor-log database; GitHub's processing is governed by its own notice.
  • Cloudflare: provides DNS and, for the custom domain, the currently observed proxy/CDN delivery layer. It processes technical traffic for delivery, caching and security. The live domain also returns NEL and Report-To headers: supporting browsers may send network-failure reports to a.nel.cloudflare.com. These are infrastructure diagnostics, separate from the site's scripts. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and Network Error Logging documentation. The provider's policy and settings govern diagnostic data and retention.
  • GitHub-hosted media and downloads: Tools loads its ACGMN-Grid example directly from raw.githubusercontent.com; this can send your IP address, browser headers and any referrer permitted by your browser before you follow a link. Original-image and wallpaper downloads use GitHub Releases and may redirect to GitHub's asset hosts when opened. Social-preview metadata also references avatars.githubusercontent.com, which preview services or browser features may fetch. These are not all same-origin resources.
  • Public data snapshots: the operator's automation fetches Bangumi statistics and GitHub repository metadata. Your browser reads the resulting local JSON, rather than calling those APIs for the counters. Links to the underlying services are separate.
  • Email: the published contact address uses Gmail. Messages pass through your provider and the operator's provider, which process their contents and metadata under their policies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not embed an advertising or social-media tracking widget. Third-party processing above still occurs without such a widget. We cannot set third-party retention periods or promise that providers collect no technical data.

16. Retention and Deletion

  • Correspondence: kept only as needed to resolve the enquiry, maintain a necessary record, prevent abuse or meet legal obligations. We periodically review whether it is still needed. You can request deletion; if lawful retention is necessary, we will explain the limitation. Email-provider backups and deletion schedules may delay final removal.
  • Theme cookies: expire 365 days after their setting is written, or earlier if you remove them. The navigation marker is read and removed on arrival, or otherwise remains subject to browser session-storage rules. Clear the site's cookies and storage to remove both.
  • Local content: unlocked fragments and temporary media state remain in the page while in use. Saved files, screenshots, browser caches and session-restored copies may persist; you control their deletion. Re-locking is not a remote wipe.
  • Infrastructure records: GitHub, Cloudflare and other providers apply their own retention rules. We cannot delete their independently controlled records on your behalf; their notices provide request channels.
  • Public contributions: commits, forks, caches and archives can preserve submitted information even after an edit or removal from the original repository. Contact us about information under our control and GitHub about its services.

17. Children and Minors

This is a general-interest personal fan site, not a service designed to collect children's information. We do not ask children to register or provide personal details. Hosting requests are still processed as explained above.

If you are a minor, involve a parent or guardian where appropriate and avoid sending unnecessary personal details. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information may contact us to request review and deletion, subject to applicable law. We do not apply a single worldwide age threshold to every visitor.

Local Interactions, Email Drafts and Clipboard

  • Contact letter: the optional name and message are used locally to build a mailto: link. Continuing opens your email application or configured handler; the site does not submit the form to a server. Your email application may save or sync a draft, and we receive it only if you send it.
  • Copy buttons: clicking a copy control asks the browser to write the displayed contact detail to your clipboard. The site does not read your clipboard. Clipboard history or device sync is controlled by your system.
  • Tools and search: the built-in concentration/volume calculator and bookmark search operate in the page without uploading inputs. This statement does not cover separately hosted tools reached through links.
  • Fortunes and effects: drawings and animations use page state and static assets; the current scripts do not send the selected fortune or animation events as analytics. Asset requests are still visible to delivery providers.
